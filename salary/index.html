Automatic holiday integration

Set your normal workdays once. Holiday premiums are estimated automatically for each saved month when a holiday falls on a scheduled workday.

Holiday calculations use the weekly schedule set in the Salary & Benefits section above.

Holiday premiumsAutomatic per monthIncluded in the corresponding 15th or month-end cutoff calculation.
Multipliers usedHoliday estimates use the scheduled hours from your Weekly Work Schedule for each holiday date. Regular holiday: 200% total rate; regular holiday on rest day: 260%; special non-working day: 130%; special non-working day on rest day: 150%. The planner shows the additional premium above regular monthly salary. Night differential adds 10% of the applicable hourly rate for hours from 10 PM to 6 AM. Special working days have no statutory holiday premium. For monthly-paid employees, this calculator adds the premium portion on top of the ordinary monthly salary, not the ordinary day's base pay again.

Holiday pay is estimated only when the automatic holiday-work assumption is enabled and a holiday falls on one of your selected scheduled workdays. Actual attendance, rest-day status, special proclamations and company payroll rules can change the result.

Unpaid absences are recorded separately inside each cutoff's expanded details. Paid leave should not be entered as an unpaid absence.
