Cloud sync & data safety

Backup & restore

Each paired browser stores its own revocable device token. Keep a separate JSON backup. The setup key is used only once and is never saved in this browser.

LOCAL PLAN
“Record sale” here is a planning action only: the listed net value moves to your bank balance and the asset is marked sold. Nothing is sold in real life. Update values when you have a new estimate; unsold assets are counted in net worth.

Planning sketch only, not financial advice. Values and sale proceeds are estimates and exclude selling costs or taxes.
